A make.fun entry for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes one 100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`:
a fantasy villain, thirty years holding the valley — tolls, tithe, and a
little bell that rings when someone lies — who gives the whole valley
away for the price of nothing and keeps only the armchair. The tiredness
lives in the pauses, the deflections, and the giving-up, never in a
plain statement that he is weary; the sting lands in the last line.
